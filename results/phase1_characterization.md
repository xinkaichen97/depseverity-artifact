## 7. Per-criterion annotations

**None.** The release is `text,label` only. There are no PHQ-9 item labels, no evidence
spans, and no annotator metadata. The C3 extraction step therefore has no gold standard
to be scored against directly — it can only be evaluated end-to-end through aggregation.
